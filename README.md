# Sales-Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline
Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard
An interactive Power BI Dashboard designed to monitor sales performance, profitability, regional distribution, and product category analysis through dynamic visualizations and business intelligence techniques.


2. Short Description / Purpose
The Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard provides a centralized view of an organization's sales data by transforming raw transactional records into meaningful business insights. It enables users to analyze Total Sales, Profit, Quantity Sold, and Profit Margin while comparing performance across product categories, regions, and states.

The dashboard incorporates interactive filters and dynamic visualizations, allowing decision-makers to identify trends, evaluate business performance, and support data-driven strategic decisions with greater efficiency.


3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
📊 Microsoft Power BI Desktop – Primary Business Intelligence platform used for designing interactive reports and dashboards.
📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, reshaping, and preparing raw sales data before analysis.
🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Implemented to create calculated measures, KPIs, Profit Margin calculations, and dynamic business metrics.
🗂️ Data Modeling – Relationships were established between tables to ensure accurate calculations and efficient report performance.
📑 Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) – Used as the primary data source containing sales transaction records.
📁 PBIX File Format (.pbix) – Used for dashboard development, modification, and report publishing.
📈 Power BI Visualizations – KPI Cards, Line Chart, Bar Chart, Donut Chart, Table, and Interactive Slicers were used to present business insights effectively.


4. Data Source
Source: Sales Dataset (Microsoft Excel)
The dashboard is based on a structured sales dataset containing approximately 3,000 sales transactions. The dataset includes information related to product categories, regions, states, sales amount, profit, quantity sold, and customer orders.

The data was cleaned and transformed using Power Query, while additional calculations such as Total Profit and Profit Margin were created using DAX measures before building the final dashboard.


📈 5. Features / Highlights
The dashboard provides an interactive and user-friendly environment for analyzing business performance through multiple visual components.

* Business Problem
Organizations often struggle to monitor sales performance across multiple regions and product categories due to scattered and complex transactional data. This dashboard addresses that challenge by consolidating all key business metrics into a single interactive report.

* Goal of the Dashboard
The primary objective of this dashboard is to provide stakeholders with a comprehensive overview of sales performance, profitability, and regional trends, enabling faster and more informed business decisions.

* Key Dashboard Features
KPI Cards displaying Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Profit Margin.
Monthly Sales Trend to monitor sales performance over time.
Sales by Category comparing Electronics, Furniture, and Office products.
Sales by Region showing sales distribution across East, West, North, and South.
Sales by State highlighting top-performing states based on total sales.
Interactive Filters (Slicers) for Category, Region, State, Sales, and Profit.
Detailed Sales Table showing Order Count, Sales, Profit, and Profit Margin for detailed analysis.

* Business Impact & Insights
Generated 8M Total Sales with 1.36M Total Profit.
Achieved an overall 17.62% Profit Margin.
Sold more than 17K products across different categories.
Electronics emerged as one of the strongest performing product categories.
Maharashtra and Delhi contributed significantly to overall sales performance.
Interactive filtering enables users to analyze data from multiple business perspectives and make informed strategic decisions.
